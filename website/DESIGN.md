# RetCom Design System

## Identity
Bold fusion of Google Antigravity IDE dark minimal tech aesthetic with early 20th century vintage Austrian poster art (Wiener Werkstätte style). Feels like a Bloomberg terminal designed by a 1920s poster artist.

## Typography
- **Headings**: Playfair Display — bold, dramatic, large.
- **Subheadings**: DM Serif Display.
- **Body and UI text**: Inter — clean modern sans-serif.
